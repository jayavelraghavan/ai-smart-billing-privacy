AI Smart Billing – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026
Developer: Jayavel Tech Lab
Contact: jayavelraghavan.usa@gmail.com

In short: your bills, shop details and settings stay on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The microphone is used only while you have it switched on. If you choose to turn on AI mode with your own Google Gemini key, the text of what you say is sent to Google to understand it.

1. Microphone

The app uses the microphone only after you tap the mic button, to turn what you say (for example "onion 20 rupees") into bill items. Speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google's). The app does not record, store or upload audio.

2. Information stored on your phone

The app saves the following only on your device: your bills and sales, the bill you are making, your shop name, address, phone number, email and logo, and your settings (language, number format and similar). This information is not sent to us. We cannot see it.

3. Optional AI mode (Google Gemini)

AI mode is off until you enter your own Google Gemini API key in the app. When AI mode is on and your phone is online, the text recognised from your speech (not audio) is sent to Google's Gemini service to work out the item names and prices. That data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms. Your key is stored only on your phone and is not included in Android backups or in the app's export file. You can turn AI mode off at any time.

4. Sharing

Bills, sales reports (PDF) and backup files are shared only when you choose to, and only with the app you pick (for example WhatsApp, Google Drive or email). What happens after that is governed by that app's own policy.

5. Internet

The app uses the internet only for AI mode. Everything else works offline.

6. Backups

You can export all app data as a single file and import it again. The file is saved or shared only where you choose. Android's own device backup may also include the app's data (except the AI key) if you have backup turned on in your phone's settings.

7. Deleting your data

You can remove bills in the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Children

The app is a business tool for shop owners and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
